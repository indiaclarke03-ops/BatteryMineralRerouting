# Demo script: 3 minutes

**Set-up:** open the live site on the Home page, full screen. Use the → key to advance.

**0:00 The question (Home hero).**
"Can trade and registry data show Chinese battery graphite being routed through a third-country plant before it reaches US battery makers? I tested it on one plant: BTR's anode factory in Indonesia."

**0:20 Why now (timeline strip).**
"In July 2025 the US tied clean-energy tax credits to who owns your suppliers: the prohibited-foreign-entity rules. In March 2026 the ITC voted against duties on Chinese anode material. So the duty lever is gone and the ownership lever is what's left. It's no longer just where the goods shipped from, but who owns the plant."

**0:45 Step 1, corridor (→).** "About 47,000 shipments of artificial graphite reached the US since 2024. Indonesia isn't even top ten. A single plant is easy to miss."

**1:00 Step 2, the plant: inferred (→).** "Sayari has five records for this one plant. Four share a street address in Kendal. That's an *inferred* link: dotted amber, labelled with our reasoning."

**1:20 Step 3, the owner: contractual (→).** "Here's the surprise: Sayari has no registry ownership for the plant. Its shortest path to BTR is a trade link. Sayari does show BTR owning a *different* Indonesian company, so this is a coverage gap. The ownership line comes from BTR's own press release, and it's labelled that way."

**1:45 Step 4, in and out: transactional (→).** "Solid blue is goods moving. Artificial graphite comes in from BTR and from a trader in the BTR Building in Shenzhen. Artificial graphite goes out to Ultium Cells, SK Battery America, StarPlus, GM and Tesla in 2025. Same product in, same product out."

**2:05 Step 5, the gap (→).** "What Sayari can't tell us: how much Chinese material is inside each US shipment, or who owns the plant. And two of Sayari's name filters silently failed."

**2:20 Method (switch to Method page).** "Every line on the site links to the exact Sayari call. Here's the scorecard: 15 calls worked, 4 partly, 5 failed. The failures are findings too."

**2:40 Verdict and ask (back to Home, scroll to Verdict).** "Detected: the trade. Suggested: the ownership. Not visible: the share of Chinese content. This isn't an accusation; it's where due diligence should look. I'd like feedback on four things, listed here."

**3:00 End.**
