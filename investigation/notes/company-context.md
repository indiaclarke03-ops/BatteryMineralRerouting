# Company context: third-country anode plants (paraphrased)

All notes are paraphrased from Tavily results gathered 2026-10-14. Call ids refer to tavily_calls.json.

## BTR New Material Group: Indonesia (PT Indonesia BTR New Energy Material)
- BTR started production in Indonesia in Aug 2024 and called it the largest anode plant outside China. Phase 1 is 80,000 tpa on a $478M investment. Phase 2 adds 80,000 tpa ($299M), for 160,000 tpa in total. (t012)
  - https://www.btrchina.com/en/News/info.aspx?itemid=1243 (BTR press release; same text at https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/btrs-indonesian-facility-becomes-largest-anode-plant-outside-china-302216431.html)
- The company has sites at both IMIP (Morowali, Central Sulawesi) and the Kendal Industrial Park (Central Java). In March 2025 BTR's CEO said Phase 2 would start production in late 2025. (t012)
  - https://www.petromindo.com/news/article/btr-confirms-its-phase-ii-anode-material-production-to-commence-in-2025
- Battery-Tech Network says BTR owns 70% and Stellar Investment 30%. This is not primary-verified. (t012)
  - https://battery-tech.net/company/pt-indonesia-btr-new-energy-material-kendal
- Whether Phase 2 actually reached production is not confirmed in these results.

## BTR New Material Group: Morocco
- In Aug 2024 BTR announced a 60,000 tpa integrated anode project in Tangier Tech City, run through its subsidiary BTR Mediterranean New Material Technology. Investment is capped at about $366M, with roughly two years of construction. (t013)
  - https://www.btrchina.com/en/News/info.aspx?itemid=1244
- Electrive reported Ford as an expected customer and a start in 2026 or 2027. BTR also has a separate 50,000 tpa cathode project in Tangier. (t013)
  - https://www.electrive.com/2024/08/26/btr-plans-to-produce-anode-materials-in-morocco
  - https://www.btrchina.com/en/News/info.aspx?itemid=1218 (cathode project)
- The Morocco anode plant's operating status in 2026 is unverified.

## Zhongke Electric (Hunan Zhongke Shinzoom): Oman
- The Sohar Freezone anode plant is planned at up to 200,000 tpa in three phases, with about $1bn of investment (Gulf News) or $1.1bn (ChemAnalyst). It would be one of the company's largest bases outside China. (t014)
  - https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/oman/oman-to-host-one-of-the-worlds-largest-lithium-ion-battery-anode-plants-at-sohar-freezone-1.500557832
  - https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/chinese-firm-plans-1-1-billion-lithium-ion-battery-anode-materials-plant-in-oman-37189
- According to GraphiteHub, the board approved the project in June 2025 and ground was broken on 8 May 2026, with production targeted for 2027. The same site says Zhongke cancelled three other planned anode projects in June 2026, in Sichuan, Gansu and Morocco. These are single-source claims that should be checked against company filings. (t014)
  - https://graphitehub.com/zhongke-electric-oman-anode-base-2027
