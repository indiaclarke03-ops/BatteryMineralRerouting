# Re-check part A, 5 Nov 2026: policy and headline facts

Scope: what is new or wrong since the 18 Oct 2026 check. All web calls were Tavily, sent one at a time. No 429 errors occurred.

As of 5 Nov 2026, China has published no notice moving the 10 Nov 2026 end date of Announcement No. 70, so that date still stands and must be re-checked after 10 Nov. The biggest finding is that the USITC negative vote on anode material is under appeal at the Court of International Trade, with argument set for the second half of January 2027. That rests on one interested company's filing, and the site currently says nothing about it. The site also misses China's separate Announcement No. 72, whose suspension of tighter graphite review for US exports ends on 27 Nov 2026, and the BIS Affiliates Rule, due back on 10 Nov 2026 unless BIS acts. Treasury has issued nothing new on PFEs. The UFLPA Entity List grew to 190 names on 29 Oct, and none of the eight companies checked is on it. There were no new 1260H notices, no new BIS Entity List additions and no new tariffs on graphite or critical minerals. On the home page, two labels need small fixes and one source can be upgraded to the IEA directly.
